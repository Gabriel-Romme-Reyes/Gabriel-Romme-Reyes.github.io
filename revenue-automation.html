Event-Driven Agentic Automation

I led the founding phase of a revenue automation platform, defining the initial services, connecting the first workflows, and introducing the orchestration foundation.

What I built

Original service architecture

Established the initial boundaries across event intake, trigger processing, action dispatch, and state management.

Early implementation

Orchestration foundation

Result

First revenue-generating IT product · 141K steps · 1.6K purchases / 30 days

Across a read-only 30-day production window, the platform executed 141,275 recorded automation steps across 45,963 customer-account relationships, peaking at 706 steps per hour and 13,006 per day. It also tracked 1,640 purchase outcomes. The purchase data is operational and mutable, so this is not presented as audited or incremental revenue. The platform became the IT team’s first revenue-generating project; I led its founding phase, and the team continued expanding it after I moved to CRM-related work.
